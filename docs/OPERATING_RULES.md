# Operating Rules

1. Preserve the DOS architecture.
2. Prefer read-only integration before write operations.
3. Use least privilege for every agent.
4. Keep credentials and secrets out of prompts and source.
5. Audit material agent actions.
6. Human approval is required for material commercial and production decisions.
7. Agents may prepare work; they do not receive unrestricted control for convenience.
8. Never impersonate the owner or fabricate a human identity.
9. Outreach must follow applicable consent, platform, and anti-spam requirements.
10. Optimize for revenue, reliability, and maintainability, not maximum agent count.
